Proposal

Plan pricing: what the platform costs us, and what we should charge

Prepared 2 October 2026 · Exchange rate used: ₦1,400 = $1 (market rate today ₦1,328, with a buffer for naira movement)

In short: today's prices do not cover what the platform costs to run. Basic at ₦5,000 a month is about $3.75, less than an academy's share of chat, email and hosting.

The proposal below is to raise Basic to ₦15,000 and Pro to ₦40,000 a month, keep Free as a trial, and keep the 5% service charge on every student payment.

In both growth scenarios below, that pays for every service we use and leaves a margin of about 43–47%, provided three simple usage limits are added.

3. Does the proposed pricing pay for itself?

What the scenarios show
  • The 5% service charge does most of the work. In scenario B, plan fees alone (₦1.05m) would not cover costs (₦1.89m). The service charge must stay on every plan.
  • Live classes are our biggest cost by far, about three quarters of costs in scenario B. They are the thing to protect with usage limits.
  • At today's prices the platform loses money. Scenario A at ₦5,000 / ₦15,000 brings in only ₦125,000 in plan fees, which doesn't even cover the ₦340,200 fixed costs.

4. Why these specific prices

Free: ₦0

With one student allowed, it works as a free trial: an academy can set up, build courses and see the product before paying. Its cost to us is close to nothing, and it brings academies in.

Basic: ₦15,000

About $11 a month for a full branded school with live classes, chat, certificates and agents. That's affordable for a small Nigerian academy: it's covered by a single student paying ₦15,000.

It also pays its fair share of our fixed costs, which ₦5,000 did not.

Pro: ₦40,000

Pro carries our three most expensive extras: pre-recorded video, AI materials and custom domains. It's aimed at academies already earning well, for whom ₦40,000 (about $30) is a small share of monthly sales.

At under 3× the price of Basic, upgrading is still an easy decision.

Enterprise: from ₦150,000

Large institutions need unlimited capacity, API access and white-label, and usually some hands-on setup. Quoting from ₦150,000 lets us price each one on its real usage instead of losing money on a flat fee.

5. Three safeguards to add

These matter as much as the prices. Each one protects us from an academy that costs more than it pays.

  1. A monthly allowance for live classes. Basic includes up to 100 learners in live classes a month and Pro up to 300. Beyond that: ₦1,000 per extra learner, or move up a plan.
    Why: without it, one Basic academy with 400 live students could cost us around ₦400,000 a month while paying ₦15,000.
  2. A minimum service charge of ₦500 per payment ("5% or ₦500, whichever is higher").
    Why: 5% of a cheap ₦5,000 course is only ₦250, which is less than that student's live classes cost us (₦460 or more).
  3. A fair-use limit on AI materials: 30 AI presentations per academy per month on Pro.
    Why: one AI subscription is shared by every academy, so a few heavy users could use up the credits for everyone.

6. How to roll it out

  • Existing academies keep their current price for 3 months, with an email explaining the change and the new value. Notice makes increases far easier to accept.
  • Annual plans get 2 months free. This brings in cash up front and keeps academies with us longer.
  • Review prices every 6 months. Most of our costs are billed in dollars, so a weaker naira squeezes the margin.
  • Prices can change without new software. They're settings, so new prices go live on the pricing page and plan cards as soon as they're changed. The three safeguards need a small amount of development, a few hours each.

7. Assumptions to confirm

  • Hosting is estimated at $40 a month at early stage and $100 at growth stage. Please replace with the actual hosting bill.
  • An average student payment of ₦25,000 a month. If our academies charge much less, the minimum service charge in safeguard 2 becomes more important.
  • Service prices were checked on 2 October 2026 and can change. The current plan level we're on with each provider should be confirmed in their dashboards. In particular, if live video is still on the free 25-learner plan, classes will stop working beyond that.
